Arky Yang@arkyyang

Beyond Prompts: Measuring and Optimizing LLM Tool-Agent Harnesses — new paper (arXiv, 4 Sep 2026; accepted EMNLP 2026). https://t.co/pKJEV3lx5R Treats harness optimization around a fixed model as budgeted selection: edits are guarded intercepts at the tool boundary, not rewrites of execution logic. PRISM clusters failures, routes each repair to a prompt, middleware, or joint surface, and picks candidates by gate pass-rate AND reliability (RelLift95), not score alone. 3 distinct takeaways for product builders: - Report selected-harness reliability, not just mean lift. BH found the best individual harnesses but had negative reliable lift on both tau-bench domains — a strong explorer, an unreliable selector. - Constrain middleware to tool-boundary edit patterns (silent correction, error blocking, prerequisite blocking). Unconstrained middleware (BH-MW) fell below its own prompt-only variant on Retail; the constraint, not the surface, is what makes middleware safely searchable. - Route each failure to the right surface: prompts for global policy, middleware for locally-checkable execution invariants. Removing routing collapsed lift first-order on all three tasks; prompts alone miss runtime failures, unconstrained middleware adds selection risk. Experiment setup (from the paper): fixed inner execution model gpt-5.4-mini, outer proposer claude-opus-4.7. Benchmarks: BFCL multi-round (600-case scorecard) and tau2-Retail / tau2-Telecom (74-case scorecard each of 114 tasks). Baselines BH, GEPA, MIPROv2 + PRISM; disjoint repair/gate/scorecard splits, 4 seeds, up to 16 runs. Baseline = native Deep Agents (v0.5.6). Metrics: mean held-out lift (pp), WorstLift, repeatability, RelLift95(B) by 5,000 bootstrap. PRISM-MW mean lifts 14.2 / 14.9 / 10.1 pp and positive RelLift95 on all three.

Beyond Prompts: Measuring and Optimizing LLM Tool-Agent Harnesses —— 新论文（arXiv，2026 年 9 月 4 日；已被 EMNLP 2026 接收）。https://t.co/pKJEV3lx5R 将固定模型周围的 harness 优化视为有预算的选择问题：编辑是在工具边界设置的受控拦截，而不是重写执行逻辑。PRISM 会聚类失败，将每个修复路由到 prompt、中间件或联合表面，并按门控通过率和可靠性（RelLift95）选择候选，而不只看分数。 给产品构建者的 3 个明确启示： - 报告所选 harness 的可靠性，而不只是平均提升。BH 找到了最佳的单个 harness，但在两个 tau-bench 领域的可靠提升为负，是强探索器，却是不可靠的选择器。 - 将中间件限制在工具边界编辑模式内（静默纠正、错误阻断、前置条件阻断）。不受约束的中间件（BH-MW）在 Retail 上低于其仅 prompt 版本；让中间件可被安全搜索的是约束，而不是表面本身。 - 将每个失败路由到正确表面：prompt 处理全局策略，中间件处理可在本地检查的执行不变量。移除路由后，三个任务的一阶提升都崩塌；仅靠 prompt 会遗漏运行时失败，不受约束的中间件会增加选择风险。 实验设置（来自论文）：固定内部执行模型 gpt-5.4-mini，外部提议器 claude-opus-4.7。基准：BFCL multi-round（600 个案例记分卡）和 tau2-Retail / tau2-Telecom（各 74 个案例记分卡，来自 114 个任务）。基线为 BH、GEPA、MIPROv2 + PRISM；修复/门控/记分卡拆分互不重叠，4 个种子，最多 16 次运行。Baseline = 原生 Deep Agents（v0.5.6）。指标：平均留出集提升（pp）、WorstLift、可重复性、通过 5,000 次 bootstrap 计算的 RelLift95(B)。PRISM-MW 的平均提升为 14.2 / 14.9 / 10.1 pp，并且在三项任务上 RelLift95 均为正。

Skill 搜到了，Agent 反而把原本会做的题做错了。 这篇《Skill Following》专门研究一个很容易会踩坑的问题。 现在评估 Agent Skill，通常会看「有 Skill」和「没 Skill」时整体分数差多少。 但这里有个偏差。Agent 往往只会在某些任务上主动检索 Skill，而这些任务本身可能就更简单或更适合检索。 作者因此提出 RAE，只看真正发生过 Skill 检索的任务，再对同一道题比较「用了 Skill」和「没用 Skill」的结果。 结果在 MBPP+ 上，DeepSeek-V3.2 按传统统计看，检索带来 +31.6 个百分点提升；换成 RAE 后却变成 -9.1。 Gemini-2.5-Flash-lite 也出现类似情况，+6.2 变成 -15.6。 这种现象在数学任务里同样存在。Llama-3.3-70B 在 Math500 上看起来提升 +14.2，真正的 RAE 却是 -39.4。 进一步分析发现，问题经常出在检索之后。 Agent 找到了 Skill，却没有真正用进去；有时理解错了，有时过度套用，还有时直接破坏了最终输出格式。 这篇给 Skill Agent 一个很重要的提醒。 「能检索到」和「能正确使用」其实是两种能力。 以后评估 Memory、Skill 或 RAG，也许都应该多问一句：这条外部知识被调用之后，究竟救回了多少任务，又弄坏了多少原本能做对的任务？ 📎 arxiv: https://t.co/ZIMqhQJNY1

What we’re witnessing right now in AI for math is a dramatic improvement in objective-driven problem-solving, but it does not speak to non-objective discovery. I have always maintained that objectives are achievable when they are one stepping stone away, which means that they are within reach if their necessary prerequisite stepping stones are already laid. We say as much in Why Greatness Cannot Be Planned. Furthermore, I have identified “the stepping stepping already laid” as every invention and idea ever hatched over the course of human history. That is the frontier of civilization. But a massive challenge for every creative field is to make all the relevant stepping stones actually accessible to its practitioners. How can anyone be aware of all of human knowledge in their field? To the extent they cannot, some dots cannot be connected even if they’re already uncovered. AI advances today have suddenly made the existing stepping stones vastly more accessible. It can see something close to “all” of them, which means it has the possibility of leaping to many more next stepping stones in the chain. Combined with a brute force-ish “try every known stepping stone under the sun”, that’s what we’re seeing in the pursuit of popular objectives. But discovery and innovation are not only about finding the right existing stepping stones to solve your objective because for many problems the stepping stones are not already laid and also do not follow directly from existing stepping stones. If those problems are ever to be solved, they will require open-ended, non-objective search through spaces of ideas that are motivated not by solving a problem as an objective, but by being interesting in their own right. The advances we’re seeing today speak only faintly to that purpose, which is why open-endedness remains firmly at the frontier of AI.

我们现在在 AI 数学领域看到的，是目标驱动型问题求解能力的大幅提升，但这并不代表非目标导向的发现能力也在进步。我一直认为，当目标只隔着一块垫脚石时，它就是可达成的，也就是说，只要必要的前置垫脚石已经铺好，它就触手可及。我们在《Why Greatness Cannot Be Planned》中也表达过类似观点。 此外，我把“已经铺好的垫脚石”定义为人类历史进程中曾经诞生过的每一项发明和每一个想法。这就是文明的前沿。但对每个创造性领域来说，一个巨大挑战是如何让从业者真正接触到所有相关的垫脚石。谁能知晓自己领域内的全部人类知识？只要做不到这一点，有些点即便已经被发现，也仍然无法连接起来。 如今的 AI 进展突然让现有垫脚石变得极大程度上更易获取。它几乎能看到“全部”垫脚石，这意味着它有可能跳到链条中更多的下一块垫脚石。再结合某种近似蛮力的“尝试天下所有已知垫脚石”的方法，这就是我们在追求热门目标时所看到的现象。 但发现和创新并不只是找到正确的现有垫脚石来解决你的目标，因为对许多问题来说，垫脚石尚未铺好，也不会直接从现有垫脚石中推导出来。如果这些问题终有一天要被解决，就需要在思想空间中进行开放式、非目标导向的搜索，其动机不是把解决某个问题当作目标，而是这些想法本身就足够有趣。我们今天看到的进展对这一目的只有很微弱的启示，因此开放性仍然稳稳处在 AI 的前沿。
