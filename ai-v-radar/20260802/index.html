It's 2027, and imagine you record a 60 sec video of a dog in the park. Now imagine you take the first frame of that video and give it to the most powerful video model ever created at that time and ask it to create a 60 sec video only with that frame. If the current pace of video models continues on the trajectory we're seeing, you can expect that by then (or in a not-too-distant future), you will, theoretically, be able to generate a video that's identical to the original 60 sec video. The generated video is equivalent to the real video. The motion, physics, movements, actions, and all possible outcomes of every possible scenario of that video are somewhere in the latents of the model. If you can find the one that represents the reality you experience then you have a generated video that's the same video you recorded. But you can also, theoretically, create every variation of your original video with any possible permutation possible. That means we have finally created a simulation of reality. The map is the territory. Now imagine it's 2028 and you can do all of this in real-time...

现在是 2027 年，想象你在公园里拍了一段 60 秒的狗狗视频。再想象你取出这段视频的第一帧，交给当时有史以来最强大的视频模型，并让它只凭这一帧生成一段 60 秒的视频。如果视频模型继续沿着我们目前看到的轨迹发展，那么到那时（或在不太遥远的未来），理论上你可以生成一段与原始 60 秒视频完全相同的视频。生成的视频等同于真实视频。那段视频中的运动、物理规律、动作、行为，以及每一种可能场景的所有可能结果，都以某种形式存在于模型的潜变量中。如果你能找到那个代表你所体验现实的潜变量，就能得到一段与你录制的视频相同的生成视频。但理论上，你也可以基于原始视频创造出任何可能排列组合下的所有变体。 这意味着我们终于创造出了现实的模拟。地图即领土。 现在想象一下，到了 2028 年，你可以实时完成这一切……
