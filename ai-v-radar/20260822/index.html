The sense of touch is the most criminally under-explored modality in robotics. Imagine doing sleight of hand wearing thick oven mitts. That's exactly how a robot feels today if it were alive. A magnetic piece snapping into place, a paper cup peeling out of a stack, a USB negotiating its way into the port - all invisible to the camera. Learning how to feel must be a full-stack co-designed effort. We are open-sourcing a principled methodology called "T-Rex": 1. Tactile as first-class citizen of the model. Our mixture-of-transformer runs two clocks asynchronously: a slow visuomotor expert plans the motion, and a fast tactile expert refines it in real time with high-frequency corrections at 4 "touch ticks" per vision tick. Forces change faster than frames arrive, so the architecture had to as well. 2. Open data. The largest tactile dataset ever released to our knowledge: a 50-hour (~5,500 episodes) high-quality, carefully synchronized robot play corpus, collected on SOTA tactile hand hardware with 22 degrees of freedom. Available today on HuggingFace! 3. Training recipe: T-Rex extends our prior work, EgoScale. Human egocentric videos for pretraining, a diverse dose of tactile robot play for mid-training. Our experiments show this bridges contact-free pretraining to contact-rich manipulation remarkably well. Pixels are cheap and everywhere, but they run out of steam at the moment of contact. Tactile will carry the last mile. The next scaling curve will be measured in hours of touch. T-Rex is a great collaboration between NVIDIA and Berkeley: 🧵

触觉是机器人学中最被严重低估、探索最少的模态。想象一下戴着厚烤箱手套表演手法戏法。如果机器人有生命，它今天的感受大概就是这样。磁性零件啪地吸合到位、纸杯从一摞杯子中剥离出来、USB 摸索着插进接口，这些对摄像头来说全都不可见。 学会“感受”必须是一次全栈协同设计的努力。我们正在开源一种原则化方法，名为“T-Rex”： 1. 将触觉作为模型的一等公民。我们的 mixture-of-transformer 异步运行两个时钟：较慢的视觉运动专家负责规划动作，较快的触觉专家则以每个视觉 tick 对应 4 个“触觉 tick”的高频校正，实时细化动作。力的变化比帧到达更快，所以架构也必须随之改变。 2. 开放数据。据我们所知，这是迄今发布的最大触觉数据集：50 小时（约 5,500 个 episode）高质量、精确同步的机器人 play 语料，采集自拥有 22 个自由度的 SOTA 触觉手硬件。今天已在 HuggingFace 上发布！ 3. 训练配方：T-Rex 扩展了我们此前的工作 EgoScale。用人类第一视角视频进行预训练，再用多样化的触觉机器人 play 进行中期训练。我们的实验表明，这能非常好地弥合无接触预训练与高接触操作之间的差距。 像素便宜且无处不在，但一到接触瞬间就力不从心。触觉将完成最后一公里。下一条 scaling curve 将以触摸小时数来衡量。 T-Rex 是 NVIDIA 与 Berkeley 的出色合作：🧵

When we first started working on Diffusion Policy, which felt like an "unlock" for fitting human demonstration data, the explanation I gave myself was that "human demonstration data was subtly much more multimodal than we thought, so we need the modeling power of denoising diffusion to fit it properly." What Michael's paper brings to light is that some of this multimodality was probably exacerbated (more than I realized) by the fact that we've only been giving our models incredibly short observation context lengths. For me, this is pretty compelling evidence that we should be pushing for policies with long-context lengths and short action prediction horizons.

我们最初开始研究 Diffusion Policy 时，它像是让人类示范数据拟合得起来的一次“解锁”。我当时给自己的解释是：“人类示范数据的多模态性比我们想象的要微妙得多，所以需要去噪扩散的建模能力，才能正确拟合它。” Michael 的论文揭示的是，这种多模态性的一部分，可能被一个事实进一步放大了（比我意识到的更严重）：我们一直只给模型极短的观察上下文长度。 对我来说，这是相当有说服力的证据，说明我们应该推动具备长上下文长度和短动作预测 horizon 的策略。

Michael Zeng@michzeng_

Action chunking — especially executing long action sequences open-loop — is widely used in imitation learning for robotic manipulation. Why is it so effective and do we really need it? We find a key reason: Long open-loop execution helps short-context policies imitate non-Markovian experts. With this insight, we show how to move beyond open-loop execution: extending policy context restores reactivity while achieving even higher task performance. 🧵(1/5)

动作分块，尤其是开环执行长动作序列，在机器人操作的模仿学习中被广泛使用。它为什么如此有效？我们真的需要它吗？我们发现了一个关键原因： 长时间开环执行有助于短上下文策略模仿非马尔可夫专家。 基于这一洞见，我们展示了如何超越开环执行：扩展策略上下文可以恢复反应性，同时实现更高的任务性能。 🧵(1/5)

vLLM@vllm_project

In RL training, a vLLM rollout engine and a Megatron trainer can run the same policy yet disagree on a token's logprob due to floating-point non-associativity. SkyRL's IsoExec combines an execution contract with a unified model, aligning rounding-sensitive execution choices across rollout and training. Bitwise parity holds across different TP, EP, and SP layouts. For Gated DeltaNet, the chunkwise-parallel recurrent algorithm makes parallel training and prefill bitwise identical to recurrent decode. Qwen3.5-35B-A3B, DAPO, 8xH100, 50 steps: logprob diff 1.6e-2 to 6.7e-7, full-step overhead 25.3% ✅ vLLM's scheduler and CUDA graphs still apply. Thanks to @JiangAlexander1 and the SkyRL team at @NovaSkyAI. 🔗 https://t.co/Y8SIIgHqAr

在 RL 训练中，vLLM rollout engine 和 Megatron trainer 可以运行同一个策略，却因浮点非结合性而对某个 token 的 logprob 得出不同结果。 SkyRL 的 IsoExec 将执行契约与统一模型结合，对齐 rollout 与训练中对舍入敏感的执行选择。 在不同 TP、EP 和 SP 布局下都能保持逐位一致。对于 Gated DeltaNet，chunkwise-parallel 循环算法让并行训练和 prefill 与循环 decode 达到逐位相同。 Qwen3.5-35B-A3B、DAPO、8xH100、50 步：logprob diff 从 1.6e-2 降至 6.7e-7，完整步骤开销 25.3% ✅ vLLM 的 scheduler 和 CUDA graphs 仍然适用。 感谢 @JiangAlexander1 以及 @NovaSkyAI 的 SkyRL 团队。 🔗 https://t.co/Y8SIIgHqAr
